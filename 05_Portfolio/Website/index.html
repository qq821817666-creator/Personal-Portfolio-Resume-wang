About / 关于

I bridge Industrial Design craftsmanship with AI-driven computational workflows to create CMF strategies that are both emotionally resonant and production-ready.

Industrial Design roots: I understand how materials behave at scale — injection molding textures, anodizing chemistry, PVD color consistency, fabric weave density. AI augmentation: I use LLM-assisted trend research, ComfyUI/GPT Image for PBR texture generation, and Python scripts for CIELAB color extraction to explore 10× more combinations than traditional CMF workflows allow.

Each project follows a five-phase closed loop: Business Context → Market & Trend Research → Concept Exploration with weighted decision matrices → Final CMF Strategy with CIELAB/Pantone/ process specifications → Production Feasibility with BOM, durability testing, and sustainability.

  • Color Systems (CIELAB, Pantone)
  • CMF Trend Forecasting (STEEP)
  • PBR Rendering (Blender / GPT Image)
  • AI Texture Generation (ComfyUI)
  • Python Color Analysis
  • CMF Spec Documentation
  • Materials: Plastics, Metals, Textiles
  • Sustainable Material Selection
  • BOM & Production Feasibility
  • Bilingual CMF Communication (EN/ZH)
  • Brand Identity Systems

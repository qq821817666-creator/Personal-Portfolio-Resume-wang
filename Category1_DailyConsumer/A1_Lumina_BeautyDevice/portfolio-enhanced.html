Phase 1 · Market & Trend Research / 市场与趋势研究

Three Demand Signals
& STEEP Trends

Three market forces shape the CMF direction. Consumers judge efficacy through material cues before purchase. Devices must be photogenic for social media. And 30–40% of purchases are gifts — packaging must convey thoughtful luxury.

Trend 1 · Social
Skinmalism / 精简护肤
Post-COVID simplification. Transparency, purity, fewer-but-better. CMF: Pearl whites, translucent finishes, clean matte surfaces — sterile but not cold. Materials that reference skincare textures.
Trend 2 · Technological
Warm Tech / 温暖科技
Medical-grade materials democratizing into consumer products. CMF: Brushed titanium replaces surgical steel. Soft-touch LSR replaces hard plastic. Warm metallic tones replace clinical chrome.
Trend 3 · Social
Ritual Objects / 仪式感物品
Wellness culture meets product design. CMF: Devices designed as ritual objects — jade green, pearl white, amber. Texture transitions slow the user down. Spa treatment, not chore.

Phase 2 · Concept Exploration / 方案探索

Three Design Pillars
& Three Schemes

Three strategic pillars emerge from research, each anchored in a core user need. Three competing CMF schemes were generated, then evaluated through a weighted matrix to surface the winner.

Pillar 1
Clinical Credibility / 临床可信度
Medical-grade materials (ISO 10993 ABS, Ti Grade 5), precision finishing (brushed concentric, diamond-cut chamfer), clean palettes with CIELAB/Pantone references.
Pillar 2
Ritual Warmth / 仪式温度
Warm metallic tones (rose gold, champagne) instead of surgical chrome. Soft-touch LSR grip at Shore 40–50A. Warm LED glow (2700–3000K) instead of cool blue.
Pillar 3
Vanity Presence / 梳妆台存在感
High-gloss ceramic-like body, jewelry-grade metal finishing, three curated color variants coordinating with different interior aesthetics. Lives on display, not in a drawer.
Scheme A
Clinical Purity / 临床纯净
"Dermatologist-grade, nothing more."

Scheme Selection / 方案选择

Why Derma-Luxury Wins
为什么肌肤奢华胜出

Scheme B (Derma-Luxury) scores highest on brand alignment. The strategy of translating skincare textures into product CMF — gloss = serum, velvet = cream, brushed Ti = precision tool — is both emotionally resonant and rationally defendable. It bridges the gap between clinical trust and self-care indulgence that no competitor occupies.

Post-Selection Refinements
定案后优化

V2 (Jade Calm) absorbs muted jade-green concept from Scheme C. V3 (Midnight Serum) added for dramatic/evening vanity aesthetic. Titanium treatment head upgraded to warm titanium — industry-first warm-toned Ti head. Charging base upgraded from ABS to zinc alloy + silicone dock for daily quality perception.

Phase 3 · CMF Strategy

Skincare,
Materialized.

Lumina translates skincare textures into product CMF — the high-gloss body recalls a luxury serum bottle, velvet silicone feels like a rich cream, brushed titanium is precision-engineered like a dermatologist's tool. Warm metallic tones add emotional heat to the clinical material base, making the device feel like self-care, not a medical procedure.

Phase 4 · Material Detail

Material Transitions
& Tactile Story

Each material transition is choreographed — cool titanium makes first contact with skin, warm velvet grip settles into the hand, ceramic-smooth body catches ambient light on the vanity. The arc of interaction slows the user down, transforming a treatment into a moment of self-care.

Phase 4 · Interaction Detail

Hidden-When-Off
& Docking Ritual

The LED halo is laser-etched into frosted PMMA — invisible when dark, a warm amber glow when illuminated. The charging base is a weighty zinc alloy cylinder with dark chrome PVD finish, its silicone dock absorbing the device with a soft, damped landing. Every interaction is designed.

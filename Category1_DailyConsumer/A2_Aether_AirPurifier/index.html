Phase 0 · Business Context / 商业背景

Biophilic Luxury at Mass-Premium亲生物奢华 · 大众精品定位

Aether competes against Dyson (tech-sculpture), Molekule (science-metal), and IKEA Starkvind (democratic fabric) in China's Tier-1 urban market. 70% of air purifier owners place the device in their living room or bedroom — yet the category's dominant CMF remains "white plastic box." Aether differentiates through furniture-grade material language — wood composite, acoustic textile, ceramic — that blends into curated interiors rather than standing out as technology.

Key Markets

Target User 目标用户 · The Conscious Nester

Female-leaning, 28–40, urban apartment dweller, household income ¥300K+. She curates her living space like a gallery — every object earns its place. She follows interior design accounts, knows oak from walnut, and chose her sofa fabric after ordering 12 swatches. The purifier must be visually quiet, use materials that coordinate with her furniture palette, and feel warm — not cold hard plastic.

Pain Points with Existing CMF 竞品CMF痛点

Dyson Pure: Iconic but gadget-like — glossy white plastic screams technology.
Molekule: Aluminum signals quality but reads cold/industrial. No textile warmth.
IKEA Starkvind: Fabric front is right direction but material quality reads "fast furniture."
Xiaomi/Coway: White cylinders designed to be hidden, not displayed.

Phase 1 · Market & Trend Research / 市场与趋势研究

Three Demand Signals & STEEP Trends三股需求信号 · 趋势分析

Post-pandemic, consumers associate clean air with natural materials (wood, wool, stone) rather than clinical ABS. The purifier category has been slow to adopt warm material language, creating a gap between what users want (furniture-like warmth) and what the market offers (appliance-white plastic).

T1

Biophilic Interiors 亲生物室内设计

Wood-fiber composites, stone-powder polymers, cork, wool felt — materials that signal "natural" even when engineered. Matte and open-pore finishes absorb light rather than reflect it. The opposite of high-gloss appliance plastic.

T2

Calm Technology 平静科技

Hidden-when-off displays. Warm-dim LEDs (2700K). Ceramic touch rings, fabric-covered controls. No screens, no blinking lights. The device communicates through material presence, not digital displays.

T3

Warm Minimalism 温暖极简 · Japandi

Warm beige replaces stark white. Textured matte replaces smooth gloss. Heathered fabrics replace flat-dyed textiles. Color palette shifts from cool grays to warm taupes, oatmeals, and moss tones.

Competitive Insight 竞争洞察

The market is split between "sculptural gadget" (Dyson) and "boring box" (Coway, Xiaomi). IKEA and Blueair gesture toward fabric but stop at "speaker grille" — not genuine furniture-grade materials. The opportunity: a purifier using furniture material language — wood composite, wool-like felt, ceramic — to blend into designed interiors. Not "a pretty appliance" but "a piece of furniture that filters air."

Phase 1 · Design Strategy Formulation

Three Design Pillars三大设计支柱

01

Furniture, Not Appliance 家具，非电器

Wood-fiber composites for the body instead of painted ABS. Textile-grade acoustic felt for the grille instead of perforated plastic. Ceramic or bamboo for touch points. Zero glossy plastic anywhere. Every surface references a domestic material — the purifier should feel made by a furniture workshop, not an electronics factory.

02

Visual Silence 视觉静默

Hidden-when-off LED (frosted PMMA reads as solid surface when unlit). Matte finishes absorb light (<5 GU across body). Color palette from interior neutrals — oak beige, slate gray, moss green. The device earns its place in the room by receding into it, not by demanding attention.

03

Tactile Harmony 触觉和谐

Ceramic control ring provides cool density under the fingertip — like hand-thrown pottery. Fabric grille is soft and textile-like, inviting touch. Warm wood-fiber body with subtle grain catches light differently across the day. Every touchpoint tells a material story.

Eliminated / 淘汰

Scheme A 科技无缝

Mineral-filled ABS monolith, sandblasted aluminum base. Cleanest "calm technology" expression but reads as "premium Xiaomi" — still unmistakably a machine. Single-material strategy feels under-designed at this price point.

Winner / 胜出

Scheme B 自然家居

"A piece of home that happens to clean air."

Weighted Score: 4.65 / 5.0 ★

Wood-fiber composite body + acoustic felt grille + ceramic ring + bamboo/cork/walnut base. Every material has a domestic reference. Three-variant strategy maps to Scandi, Japandi, and Organic interior styles. Directly delivers "furniture, not appliance" positioning.

Eliminated / 淘汰

Scheme C 大地雕塑

"An art object for clean air."

Weighted Score: 3.25 / 5.0

Terrazzo body + satin brass PVD + smoked oak base. Maximum visual impact but BOM exceeds target by 36%. Bold art-object aesthetic contradicts "visual silence" brand promise. Terrazzo scrap rate and brass fingerprinting are production risks.

Weighted Decision Matrix · 5 Criteria × 3 Schemes · Scheme B: Natural Home Wins — 4.65 / 5.0

Why Natural Home Wins 为什么自然家居胜出

Scheme A (Tech Seamless) was cost-efficient and clean but reads as "premium Xiaomi" — still a machine at this price point. Scheme C (Sculptural Earth) had maximum visual impact but BOM exceeded target by 36%, terrazzo scrap rates threatened yield, and brass fingerprinting contradicted "visual silence." Scheme B (Natural Home) scores highest on brand alignment — the strategy of using furniture material language (wood composite, acoustic felt, ceramic, natural bases) is both emotionally resonant and rationally defendable. It bridges the gap between design-conscious interiors and air purification that no competitor occupies.

Material Narrative

Five Zones, One Domestic Language 五区材质 · 一组家居语言

Four materials, four temperatures, one coherent domestic language. Each material zone references a furniture or interior material — wood-fiber composite recalls Scandinavian cabinetry, heathered felt recalls upholstery textiles, ceramic ring recalls hand-thrown pottery, and the natural base ring (bamboo, walnut, or cork per variant) grounds the object in genuine domestic materials. Every transition is a material conversation, not a seam. The LED diffuser is laser-etched into frosted PMMA — when off, it reads as a solid ceramic-like surface; when on, a warm amber glow (2700K) breathes through the micro-dot matrix.

01
Body Shell 机身外壳
  • ABS + Wood Fiber / Stone Powder
  • PCR Content ≥30–40%
  • MT-11020 / MT-11030 Mold Texture
  • 3–5 GU @ 60° · No Post-Spray
  • ΔE ≤ 1.5 across batches
  • Warm, Subtle Grain, Organic
02
Front Grille 前面网
  • rPET Acoustic Felt / Wool Blend
  • GRS Certified · 70–80% Post-Consumer
  • Needle-Punched · 380–400g/m²
  • Heathered Fiber Mix · 4mm Thick
  • Flame Retardant (Halogen-Free)
  • Soft, Textile, Acoustic Dampening
03
Control Ring 控制环
  • ZrO₂ Ceramic (Yttria-Stabilized)
  • Powder Injection Molded + Sintered
  • Matte Glaze · As-Fired Surface
  • Ra 0.8–1.2μm · ~8H Hardness
  • Variant-Specific Tones
  • Cool, Dense, Pottery-Like
04
Base Ring 底部环
  • V1: Bamboo Composite · FSC · 220 Grit
  • V2: Walnut Veneer · FSC 100% · 240 Grit
  • V3: Cork Composite · FSC · Raw Surface
  • All: Open-Pore · No Lacquer · 1–4 GU
  • Grounding Material — Furniture Leg Reference
05
LED Diffuser LED散光板
  • Frosted PMMA · UV-Stabilized Optical Grade
  • Laser-Etched Micro-Dot Matrix
  • Hidden-When-Off: Reads as Solid Ceramic
  • V1/V2: 2700K Warm Amber Glow
  • V3: 3000K Soft White Glow
  • Ambient Light, Not Notification

Phase 4 · Production Feasibility

BOM & Sustainability 成本与可持续性

Total CMF BOM lands at ¥174.50/unit — ¥5.50 under the ¥180 target. The cost structure reflects the premium material strategy: ceramic and fabric together account for 47% of BOM, reflecting their role as primary user-facing surfaces. A cost-down roadmap at 100K+ volume brings BOM to ¥164.20 through ceramic process optimization and fabric sourcing at scale. Sustainability is embedded in material selection rather than bolt-on: 30–40% PCR in body shells, GRS-certified recycled PET felt, FSC-certified natural bases, and zero EPS foam packaging.

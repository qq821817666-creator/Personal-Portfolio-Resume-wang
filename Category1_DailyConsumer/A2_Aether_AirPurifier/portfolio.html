Material Transitions

Phase 4 · Material Macros

Acoustic Felt
& Wood-Fiber Composite

The recycled PET felt grille is needle-punched at 400g/m² with heathered fiber mixing — oatmeal flecks create depth against the flat weave. The ABS + 30% wood-fiber body uses MT-11020 fine matte texture: visible wood particles embedded in warm beige polymer, like finely sanded oak.

Phase 4 · Interaction Detail

Hidden-When-Off
& Ceramic Touch

The LED indicator is laser-etched into frosted PMMA — when off, it reads as a solid ceramic-like surface. When on, a warm amber glow (2700K) breathes through the micro-dot matrix. The control ring is yttria-stabilized zirconia — cool, dense, and smooth, with the weight of hand-thrown pottery.

Project Overview

Rugged Precision坚固的精确性

Trailux is a premium outdoor lighting brand at the intersection of alpine performance and refined design. It differentiates through geological material language — not a plastic gadget strapped to your head, but an instrument carved from the mountain's own palette.

Key Markets
01

Geological Provenance 地质起源

Stone-textured housings reference alpine granite. Anodized aluminum in mineral tones. Colors extracted from alpine photography — granite gray, moss green, glacial blue. Every material traces back to the mountain.

02

Functional Finishes 功能性表面

No decoration without function. Matte = glare reduction. Stone texture = scratch resistance + grip. Laser-etched ring = rotational control. Glow button = bivouac findability. Reflective thread = visibility.

03

Lightweight Honesty 轻量诚实

Under 78g. Aluminum stays only where heat dissipation justifies weight. Polymer optimized to minimum wall. rPET elastic headband — 40% lighter than neoprene. Nothing superfluous. Every gram accounted for.

Phase 2 · Concept Exploration

Three Schemes, One Strategy三方案探索 · 一方胜出

Eliminated

Scheme A 纯技术派

"Every gram accounted for. Nothing else."

Weighted Score: 3.10 / 5.0

All-black glass-filled nylon + silver aluminum. Lightest and cheapest, but zero differentiation from Petzl/Black Diamond. All-black segment is saturated. Cannot justify ¥400+ pricing against ¥200 generics.

Winner

Scheme B 生物机能

"Carved by the mountain."

Weighted Score: 4.70 / 5.0 ★

Stone-textured body + anodized aluminum in mineral tones + lichen-inspired high-vis accents. Three variants map to alpine environments. Every finish serves a function. Unique geological material language in the category.

Eliminated

Scheme C 城市跨界

"From trailhead to coffee shop."

Weighted Score: 3.05 / 5.0

Soft-touch beige + rose gold PVD + cream band. BOM exceeds target by 15%. Soft-touch coating degrades with sunscreen. Pastel fashion aesthetic contradicts alpine-purist identity. Cotton-blend band = cold-weather safety risk.

Material Narrative

Five Zones, One Alpine Language五区材质 · 一组高山语言

Stone-textured PC/ABS body (MT-11030), clear-anodized aluminum lens ring with laser-etched grip, glow-in-dark silicone button, glass-filled nylon clip, and recycled elastic jacquard headband with reflective tracer — each zone has a functional justification and a geological reference.

Housing Body 外壳主体

  • MT-11030 / VDI 24 / MT-11010
  • 2-3 GU · In-Mold Texture · No Coating
  • UV-Stabilized · -20°C Impact Rated

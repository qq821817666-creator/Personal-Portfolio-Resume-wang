Phase 2 · Concept Exploration

Three Schemes
Bio-Rugged Wins

Pure Technical (3.10) was all-black, functional but invisible among competitors. Urban Crossover (3.05) pushed fashion-forward pastels that couldn't survive above the treeline. Bio-Rugged (4.70) won decisively — stone textures, mineral anodizing, lichen-inspired accents. Every finish serves a function and tells a geological story.

Phase 4 · Material Detail

Five Zones
One Alpine Language

Stone-textured PC/ABS body (MT-11030), clear-anodized aluminum lens ring with laser-etched grip, glow-in-dark silicone button, glass-filled nylon clip, and recycled elastic jacquard headband with reflective tracer — each zone has a functional justification and a geological reference.

Phase 4 · Functional Materials

Aluminum Lens Ring
& Glow-in-Dark Button

The only metal zone is also the only zone where metal is functionally justified — aluminum dissipates LED heat 100x faster than polymer. Laser-etched concentric grip at 0.3mm pitch enables one-handed focus adjustment. The button uses strontium aluminate phosphorescent pigment — findable at 3am in a bivouac.

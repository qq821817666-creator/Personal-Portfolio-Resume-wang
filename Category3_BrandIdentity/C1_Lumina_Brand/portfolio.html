Brand Philosophy / 品牌哲学

Where Dermatology
Meets Desire

Lumina bridges the white-coat credibility of clinical dermatology with the warm sensuality of self-care ritual. The visual identity system translates this duality into every touchpoint — precise enough for a medical brand, warm enough for the vanity.

●

Clinical Credibility临床可信度

Precision geometry, medical-grade material cues, evidence-backed color choices. Every visual element signals scientific rigor without sterility.

◆

Ritual Warmth仪式温度

Rose gold accents, soft gradients, untimed luxury. The brand breathes slowly — self-care as an unhurried daily ceremony, not a clinical chore.

■

Smart Transparency智慧透明

Ingredient-level honesty, no pseudoscience. Clean typography and clear information hierarchy reflect the brand's commitment to radical transparency.

Design Process / 设计过程

From Three Directions
to One Identity

Three competing logo concepts were explored against the brand pillars. Each direction amplified a different dimension of the Derma-Luxury proposition — then a weighted matrix surfaced the winner.

Concept A
◆

Clinical Precision临床精密

Geometric diamond motif. Sharp angles, medical crosshair reference, sterile white + cool silver. Strong on credibility — but lacks warmth, reads as pharmaceutical rather than self-care.

Concept B
✦

Botanical Bloom植萃绽放

Petal-like radiating forms. Organic curves, jade green accents, botanical serum references. Strong on warmth — but loses clinical precision, skews too natural/supplement.

Selected
●

Circle & Arc圆与弧

Intersecting circle geometry. The outer ring = clinical boundary, inner arc = focused light energy, center dot = active treatment. Clean geometry, emotional warmth, scalable to favicon.

Brand Applications / 品牌应用

From Box to Feed
从包装到内容

Brand identity comes alive across physical and digital touchpoints. Four key applications demonstrate how the Lumina system scales from intimate unboxing to public campaign.

Application 01
Product Packaging / 产品包装

FSC-certified kraft board with rose gold foil-stamped logo. Sugarcane fiber molded interior tray. Seed-embedded thank-you card. Zero plastic — every unpackaging layer tells the sustainability story.

Application 02
Quick-Start Guide / 快速入门指南

Bi-fold instruction card on recycled uncoated paper. Minimal icon system with numbered steps. Two-color print — Indigo text + Rose Gold accents. Compact enough to fit inside the packaging box.

Application 03
Campaign Poster / 营销海报

Lifestyle campaign visual for social media and retail display. Editorial beauty photography direction — soft natural light, warm tones, the device held like a precious object. Logo and tagline anchored at center with generous negative space.

Application 04
Brand Pattern / 品牌纹样

Derived from the treatment head's concentric circle geometry. The repeating ring pattern works as packaging tissue wrap, shopping bag liner, Instagram story background, or website texture. Subtle, atmospheric, unmistakably Lumina.

Project Overview

Warm Tech + Neo-Sensorial温暖科技 × 超感体验

AURA is the first premium headphone brand to synthesize Warm Tech tactility with Neo-Sensorial color intelligence — headphones that feel like a natural body extension while expressing a forward-looking, digitally-native color language.

Key Markets
01

Tactile Intelligence 触感智能

Soft-touch matte body warm to the touch, vegan leather cushions that breathe, brushed metal arms that match body temperature. No sharp edges. No cold plastic shock.

02

Dynamic Color 动态色彩

Gradient anodized aluminum. Holographic foil inlays. Colors sampled from digital-native palettes but executed with material sophistication — not screen-glow garishness.

03

Visible Integrity 可见诚实

If it's recycled, show it. If it's metal, let it be metal. No fake wood, fake metal, fake leather. Material honesty is the ultimate premium signal.

Phase 3 · Final CMF Strategy

Warm Prism: Three Color Variants暖棱镜 · 三色方案

Three variants span a gradient of adventurousness — from warm-sand accessibility to midnight-gradient boldness to arctic-violet cool. The six-zone material strategy uses genuine aluminum, bio-based vegan leather, and textile integration across all variants.

Recycled Content

🌱
Bio-Based

Bio-PU vegan leather (30% castor oil) across all variants. 35% lower carbon footprint than petroleum PU. OEKO-TEX certified.

Modular design — ear cushions replaceable. FSC molded pulp. Fabric pouch replaces plastic bag. 37% below category CO₂ avg.

Phase 3 · CMF Strategy

Warm Prism
Hybrid CMF Language

AURA synthesizes Warm Tech tactility with Neo-Sensorial color intelligence. The headphones feel warm and natural against the body — soft-touch surfaces, vegan leather that breathes, brushed metal that matches body temperature. But the color language is forward-looking: gradient anodizing on the hero variant, holographic light-catch accents, colors drawn from digital-native palettes rendered with material sophistication.

Phase 4 · Material Detail

Gradient Anodized Aluminum
& Holographic Accent

The V2 Midnight Prism earcup uses a gradient anodizing process — dark navy at the top flowing into deep teal at the bottom edge. Brushed #320 concentric lines are visible beneath the anodize. The control ring features holographic PET film inlaid under optical PMMA: aurora green shimmer that shifts blue→green→violet as the wearer moves. A category-first material expression.

Phase 4 · Interaction Detail

The Control Ring
Holographic Under PMMA

Holographic PET · Optical PMMA

The control ring is a study in depth. A holographic PET film is precision-inserted under 1mm of optical-grade PMMA. The aurora green shimmer shifts through blue and violet depending on viewing angle — the ring is never one color. An edge-lit 4000K LED channel traces the perimeter, glowing softly through the PMMA. Laser-etched markings for play/pause, volume, and ANC mode remain legible through the holographic layer.

Phase 5 · Context

Worn & Displayed

Headphones are worn visibly — on Zoom calls, in coffee shops, around the neck between listening sessions. The earcups face outward in the neck-worn state: this is where the CMF earns its place. The gradient catches room light. The holographic ring shifts as the wearer turns their head. The copper PVD gleams against a sweater. These are objects of intention — not anonymous black plastic, but headphones as an accessory worth wearing.

## Design Brief / 设计简报

Design a CMF strategy for a 2027-2028 premium NEV (New Energy Vehicle) interior. The strategy must prioritize sustainability while maintaining premium perception. Target: environmentally-conscious premium buyers in China and Europe (25-45 age).

为2027-2028年高端新能源汽车内饰设计CMF策略。策略需以可持续性为优先，同时保持高端感知。目标：中国和欧洲的环保意识高端消费者（25-45岁）。

### NEV Interior CMF Megatrends 2026-2028

#### Trend 1: Bio-Luxury / 生物奢华

## Phase 2: CMF Strategy — Selected Direction

### Direction: Bio-Luxury + Recycled Refined Hybrid

A two-tier strategy that pairs visible bio-based materials on high-touch surfaces with refined recycled materials on structural components. This creates a hierarchy of material experience — the surfaces you touch most are the most natural and premium.

### Interior CMF Breakdown

#### Component Map (Driver's perspective)

| Zone | Touch Frequency | Material Strategy |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Steering Wheel** | Continuous | Mycelium leather wrap, bio-resin center badge, backside recycled aluminum spokes |
| **Seat** | High | ECONYL 3D-knit zero-waste cover, mycelium bolsters, natural latex foam |
| **Door Panel (upper)** | Medium | FSC ash wood inlay, recycled Alcantara insert |
| **Door Panel (armrest)** | Continuous | Mycelium leather, heated in winter |
| **Dashboard (upper)** | Low-medium | Recycled Alcantara wrap, ambient light-friendly color |
| **Dashboard (lower)** | Occasional | PCR polypropylene, fine Mold-Tech texture |
| **Center Console** | High | Recycled aluminum trim, mycelium armrest surface |
| **Headliner** | Occasional | ECONYL recycled knit, acoustic-enhancing |
| **Floor / Mats** | High | SEAQUAL recycled yarn carpet, natural rubber backing |

## Phase 3: Sustainability Integration

### Material Provenance & Lifecycle

For each material, document the sustainability story:

| Material | Source | Certifications | End-of-Life | Carbon Footprint vs Conventional |
|----------|--------|---------------|-------------|----------------------------------|
| Mycelium Leather | Mushroom mycelium, agricultural waste | USDA BioPreferred | 100% compostable | -90% vs animal leather |
| ECONYL Yarn | Recovered ocean fishing nets, carpet waste | GRS, Oeko-Tex | Infinitely recyclable | -80% vs virgin nylon |
| FSC Wood | Certified European forests | FSC 100% | Biodegradable | -50% vs tropical hardwood |
| Recycled Aluminum 6061 | Post-industrial scrap | ASI, Cradle-to-Cradle | Infinitely recyclable | -95% vs virgin aluminum |
| PCR Polypropylene | Post-consumer packaging waste | GRS, EU Recycled Content | Mechanically recyclable | -70% vs virgin PP |
| Natural Latex Foam | Rubber tree sap | FSC, GOLS | Biodegradable | -60% vs PU foam |

### Design for Disassembly

Key principle: All materials must be separable for end-of-life recycling.
- Mechanical fasteners preferred over adhesives
- Mono-material assemblies where possible
- QR code on hidden surface linking to disassembly guide
- Material passport document for the vehicle
